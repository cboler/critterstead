Meet Pip, your curious Brindlekin. Good food, a little practice, and time together are the start of something wonderful.

01 · Make yourself at home

Use WASD or the arrow keys to walk. You can also click the ground or use the touch arrows. Walk close to a place, then press E or choose an action.

02 · Grow together

Feed Pip, try the hoop, and plant a little feed crop. During training, press Space in the green patch. Three well-timed beats make a good session.

03 · Follow your curiosity

The east gate leads to Clover Glade. Gather berries while Pip watches. With experience, Pip learns to harvest when asked, then helps independently.

04 · Come back with a story

Sell berries at the honesty stall, mend the shed, or try the Clover Cup. Sleep at the cottage to begin another day. Your game saves automatically in this browser.

One full day lasts about 30 minutes; activities also take time. Menus and hidden tabs pause the clock. Esc pauses · J opens your journal.
