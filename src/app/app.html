Meet {{ companion().name }}, your {{ kindName(companion().speciesId).toLowerCase() }} companion. Good food, a little practice, and time together are the start of something wonderful.

01 · Make yourself at home

Use WASD or the arrow keys to walk. On a touch screen, hold a finger on the world and your rancher walks toward it; tap to walk to a spot (the mouse works the same way). Walk close to a place, then press E or choose an action. Results appear just above the actions; stats live in {{ companion().name }}’s details panel. Scroll, pinch, or press + and − to zoom the camera.

02 · Grow together

Feed {{ companion().name }}, try the hoop, and plant a little feed crop. During training, press Space in the green patch. Three well-timed beats make a good session.

03 · Follow your curiosity

The east gate leads to Clover Glade; the west road goes down to Oakhaven and the Colosseum grounds beyond it. Gather berries while {{ companion().name }} watches. With experience, {{ companion().name }} learns to harvest when asked, then helps independently.

04 · Come back with a story

Sell berries at the honesty stall, mend the shed, or try the Clover Cup. Four garden beds grow feed, turnips and wheat in their seasons, but only while watered; water lasts until dawn and rain waters them for you. Step inside the cottage to read the wall calendar. Sleep at the cottage to begin another day, or rest together at the nook: two game hours restores up to 30 of your energy and 35 for {{ companion().name }}. Food eases hunger; rest does not. Independent foraging keeps 20 energy in reserve, while your cues can spend it. Your game saves automatically in this browser.

One full day lasts about 30 minutes; activities also take time. Menus and hidden tabs pause the clock. Esc pauses · J opens your journal. With a controller, use the left stick to move, A to act or cue {{ companion().name }}, the D-pad to choose actions or menu buttons, B to close, X for help, Y for the journal, and Menu to pause. Keyboard and touch controls still work.
